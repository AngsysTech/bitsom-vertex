## 1 Examination window
Semester 5 end-semester examinations will be held from 2026-11-30 through 2026-12-10.

## 2 Weightage
The end-semester examination carries 40% weight in every Semester 5 course. Quizzes carry 20% in total and the mid-semester examination carries 40%.
