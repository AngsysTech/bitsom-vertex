## 1 Scope
This circular applies to every B.E. Computer Science student whose expected graduation date is May 2028 or later.

## 2 Revised requirement
The Discipline Electives requirement is a minimum of 5 courses totaling at least 20 units for every student graduating from May 2028 onward.

## 3 Transition
This rule replaces the Discipline Electives sentence in Academic Handbook 2026–27 Section 3.2 for the population stated above. All other graduation buckets remain unchanged unless amended by a later circular.
