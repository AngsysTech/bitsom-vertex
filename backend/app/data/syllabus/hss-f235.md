## Unit 1: Technology and institutions
### Technology adoption
Technology adoption is treated as a named assessable topic in this unit, with definitions, worked examples, and problem-solving practice.
Reading: Everett M. Rogers, Diffusion of Innovations (5th ed.) — the adoption curve and what speeds or slows adoption.

### Institutions and incentives
Institutions and incentives is treated as a named assessable topic in this unit, with definitions, worked examples, and problem-solving practice.
Reading: Douglass C. North, Institutions, Institutional Change and Economic Performance — institutions as the rules of the game.

### Public infrastructure
Public infrastructure is treated as a named assessable topic in this unit, with definitions, worked examples, and problem-solving practice.
Reading: Brett M. Frischmann, Infrastructure: The Social Value of Shared Resources — infrastructure as a shared resource.

## Unit 2: Ethics
### Privacy
Privacy is treated as a named assessable topic in this unit, with definitions, worked examples, and problem-solving practice.
