## Unit 1: Automata
### Deterministic finite automata
Deterministic finite automata is treated as a named assessable topic in this unit, with definitions, worked examples, and problem-solving practice.
Reading: Sipser, Introduction to the Theory of Computation (3rd ed.), Ch. 1 Regular Languages, §1.1 Finite Automata.

### Nondeterministic finite automata
Nondeterministic finite automata is treated as a named assessable topic in this unit, with definitions, worked examples, and problem-solving practice.
Builds on: Deterministic finite automata.
Reading: Sipser, Introduction to the Theory of Computation (3rd ed.), Ch. 1 Regular Languages, §1.2 Nondeterminism.

### Regular expressions
Regular expressions is treated as a named assessable topic in this unit, with definitions, worked examples, and problem-solving practice.
Builds on: Nondeterministic finite automata.
Reading: Sipser, Introduction to the Theory of Computation (3rd ed.), Ch. 1 Regular Languages, §1.3 Regular Expressions.
