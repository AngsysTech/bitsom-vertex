## Unit 1: Probability
### Conditional probability
Conditional probability is treated as a named assessable topic in this unit, with definitions, worked examples, and problem-solving practice.
Reading: Blitzstein & Hwang, Introduction to Probability (2nd ed.), Ch. 2 Conditional Probability.

### Bayes theorem
Bayes theorem is treated as a named assessable topic in this unit, with definitions, worked examples, and problem-solving practice.
Builds on: Conditional probability.
Reading: Blitzstein & Hwang, Introduction to Probability (2nd ed.), Ch. 2 Conditional Probability — Bayes' rule and the law of total probability.

### Random variables
Random variables is treated as a named assessable topic in this unit, with definitions, worked examples, and problem-solving practice.
Reading: Blitzstein & Hwang, Introduction to Probability (2nd ed.), Ch. 3 Random Variables and Their Distributions.

## Unit 2: Distributions
### Discrete distributions
Discrete distributions is treated as a named assessable topic in this unit, with definitions, worked examples, and problem-solving practice.
Builds on: Random variables.
Reading: Blitzstein & Hwang, Introduction to Probability (2nd ed.), Ch. 3 Random Variables and Their Distributions and Ch. 4 Expectation — the named discrete distributions.

### Continuous distributions
Continuous distributions is treated as a named assessable topic in this unit, with definitions, worked examples, and problem-solving practice.
Builds on: Random variables.
Reading: Blitzstein & Hwang, Introduction to Probability (2nd ed.), Ch. 5 Continuous Random Variables — uniform, normal and exponential.

### Expectation and variance
Expectation and variance is treated as a named assessable topic in this unit, with definitions, worked examples, and problem-solving practice.
Builds on: Discrete distributions, Continuous distributions.
Reading: Blitzstein & Hwang, Introduction to Probability (2nd ed.), Ch. 4 Expectation.
