## Unit 1: Network foundations
### Layered architecture
Layered architecture is treated as a named assessable topic in this unit, with definitions, worked examples, and problem-solving practice.
Reading: Kurose & Ross, Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach (8th ed.), Ch. 1 Computer Networks and the Internet, §1.5 Protocol Layers and Their Service Models.

### Packet switching
Packet switching is treated as a named assessable topic in this unit, with definitions, worked examples, and problem-solving practice.
Builds on: Layered architecture.
Reading: Kurose & Ross, Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach (8th ed.), Ch. 1 Computer Networks and the Internet, §1.3 The Network Core — packet switching versus circuit switching.

### Delay and throughput
Delay and throughput is treated as a named assessable topic in this unit, with definitions, worked examples, and problem-solving practice.
Builds on: Packet switching.
Reading: Kurose & Ross, Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach (8th ed.), Ch. 1 Computer Networks and the Internet, §1.4 Delay, Loss, and Throughput in Packet-Switched Networks.
